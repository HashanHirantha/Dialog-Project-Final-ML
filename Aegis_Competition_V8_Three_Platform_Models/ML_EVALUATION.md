# V4 Periodic ML Evaluation — Fictional Dataset Only

**Goal:** prioritize **accounts that need human access review** each quarter, not stream real-time intrusion alarms. Human approvals and review policy always take precedence over model output.

The dataset contains 1,600 **fully synthetic** account-cycle examples. The Isolation Forest fit uses labeled-normal training examples and group-disjoint held-out accounts; those labels were generated by code, not verified by company auditors. Group leakage is avoided in this synthetic setup, but that does not establish real-world generalization.

After adding three separate 30-day activity features, the anomaly model's held-out statistics were approximately: ROC-AUC 0.9798 and average precision 0.9315. These evaluate **model-only novelty ranking on synthetic labels**, NOT the end-to-end access-review queue.

## Actual account-review queue performance

| Budget cap | Rules-only recall | Hybrid recall | Hybrid selected | Hybrid missed labeled cases |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10% | 0.4598 | 0.4598 | 40/400 | 47/87 |
| 20% | 0.7701 | 0.7471 | 80/400 | 22/87 |

**Conclusion:** The hybrid model is not better than the transparent rule baseline on this synthetic queue test. At 20%, it is slightly worse. Never infer a real 80% review-effort reduction or safe auto-approval from these numbers.

## Review backlog and evidence uncertainty

The 10% queue also leaves 62 **deferred candidates** and 54 accounts whose use evidence is **insufficient**. These are not safely approved or certified by the model and require a policy for further handling. Missing activity events without attested coverage cannot establish inactivity.

## Scale exercise

`python -m app.ml.competition_demo --accounts 80000 --budget 10 --with-ml` returned an in-memory synthetic account ranking in about 1.23 seconds in the authoring environment. This excludes SQL event aggregation time, production API overhead, network, persistence, and reviewer workflows. It is not a company load test.

## Measures to collect with independent business-reviewed labels

- Recall@budget, precision@budget, and false-negative count for accounts requiring owner follow-up.
- Review queue size versus true queue candidates and unknown-evidence backlog.
- Actual reviewer minutes saved, compared with blind/full review.
- False-positive investigations and business-critical accounts incorrectly skipped.
- Performance by platform, service vs human account type, application-owner groups and review cycle (future quarter holdout).
- Difference between rules-only and ML-assisted queue at the **same capacity**.

Train and evaluate on anonymized, properly authorized records before offering an automated shortlist as a replacement for enterprise certification obligations.
